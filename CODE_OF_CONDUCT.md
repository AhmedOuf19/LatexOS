# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project should be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a bug, or
propose a change without being made to feel unwelcome — regardless of experience
level, background, identity, or first language.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person.
- Remember that many users here are new to LaTeX, to Python, or to open source.
  "Obvious" questions are welcome; answering them kindly is a contribution.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change that does not fit the project's
  scope, and say so without hard feelings on either side.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, or misrepresenting what a change does.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer
([@AhmedOuf19](https://github.com/AhmedOuf19)) privately. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate
this policy, and may block repeat offenders.
